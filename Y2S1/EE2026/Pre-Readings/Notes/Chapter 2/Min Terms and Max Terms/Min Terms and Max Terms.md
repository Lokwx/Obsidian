Minterm: AND term that contains all variables of the function
> e.g ABC, !ABC
> AC, AB (Not minterms)

Maxterm: OR term that contains all variables of the function
> A + B + C, A + !B + !C

SOP → Sum of only minterms (AND terms that contains all variables)
>ABC + BCA + ABC etc

Canonical form 
> SOP (Sum of products) and POS (Product of sums)

Non Canonical form
> SOP and POS (But it doesnt contain maxterm/minterm respectively)
