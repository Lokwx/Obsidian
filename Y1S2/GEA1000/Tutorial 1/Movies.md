Numerical
1) number
2) directly quantifiable
3) distance
	- ProductionBudget
	- WorldwideGross
	- DomesticGross
	- DomesticGrossOpening
	- Duration
	- IMDbRating


Categorial
1) Nominal - no hierarchy, groups sharing similar characteristics 
	1) food = vege, meat...
	2) Names (not unique)

2) ordinal --> there is order
	1) educational level - psle, o/n lvl...
	2) Likert Scale (Strongly agree, agree .... strongly disagree)/
	   (1 2 3 4 5) --> not numerical, because 2 does not indicate that 1 is not "more" disagreeable than 2 (particularly opinions) --> IMDbRating
	

Identifiers
1) Identifies one unique object e.g title (no ambiguity)
	- Title
	- IC Number
	- Matric No
	- Student email ID
	- Car plate
	- Passport number
