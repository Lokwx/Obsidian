## Road safety for drivers 

Context:
Although the Asia Pacific region has made significant progress in road safety, with the number of road deaths decreasing for all age groups below 64 from 2000 to 2019, there has been a gradual increase in road deaths among older persons (aged 65 and above), resulting from increased vulnerability to injury in a crash as age increases.

## Traffic congestion and bottlenecks

#### Existing solutions: 
Smart transport systems 
	- adaptive traffic light signal control
	- managed motorways 

Benefits
Enhance the efficiency of existing infrastructure without requiring the costly expansion of road capacity 
Smart transport systems prioritize cost reductions through preventative maintenance, efficient energy utilization and accident prevention
This not only enhances economic sustainability, but it also fosters a greener and more sustainable environment
